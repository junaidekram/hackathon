The Pitch Manifesto

Pitches start Sun, Aug 9 at 9:30 a.m. Slides are optional, but clarity is mandatory.

Required Content

  1. The Idea: What was your original concept?
  2. The Goal: What were you trying to achieve?
  3. The Process: What steps did you take to reach the goal?
  4. Self-Assessment: How well do you think you did?
  5. The "Why": Why should your project win?
  6. Walkthrough: A live demo of the project.
  7. Q&A: Answer questions from the judges.

Presentation Style

  • Team Effort

    Every member must participate equally.

  • Engagement

    Maintain eye contact. Do not read directly off a paper.

  • Preparation

    Minimal stuttering. Know your lines.
